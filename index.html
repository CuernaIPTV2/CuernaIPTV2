IPTV FAQ

What is IPTV?

  • TV delivered through your internet connection instead of cable or satellite.

How do I watch it?

  • This depends on many factors. Usually via a TV box or app such as TiviMate, IPTVSmarters, M‑Tivi, Sparkle TV, etc.

Is it like Netflix?

  • Not exactly — IPTV usually includes live TV plus on‑demand movies and shows.

Does this page provide IPTV service?

  • No. This page is informational only and does not host or sell any channels.
