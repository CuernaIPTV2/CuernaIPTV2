IPTV FAQ

What is IPTV?

  • TV delivered through your internet connection instead of cable or satellite.

How do I watch it?

  • This is something that can be dependent upon many different factors . It is usually done via a TV box or another device, and use various different programs such as TiviMate, IPTVSmarters, M-Tivi, Sparkle TV, etc.

Is it like Netflix?

  • Not exactly, IPTV usually includes live TV plus on‑demand movies and shows.

Does this page provide IPTV service?
